Below is a detailed cooking instruction for your reference:

以下是详细的烹饪指引，供您参考：

Heating Instructions: Heat to an internal temperature of 165°F or above. Handle with care: prepared buns will be hot.

Pan-Fry Method:

  1. Add 2 tablespoons of cooking oil.
  2. Place 5 frozen buns evenly in the pan.
  3. Add ½ cup of water.
  4. Cover and cook over medium-high heat for ~10 minutes.
  5. Uncover and fry 1–2 minutes until golden.
  6. Optional: flip and fry the other side.
  7. Ensure internal temperature ≥165°F (74°C).

加热说明：使用食品温度计确认内部温度 ≥165°F。

平底锅煎制方法：

  1. 加入约 2 汤匙食用油。
  2. 放入约 5 个冷冻煎包。
  3. 加入约 1/2 杯水。
  4. 盖盖，中高火加热约 10 分钟。
  5. 开盖煎至底部金黄。
  6. 可选：翻面再煎。
  7. 确认内部温度 ≥165°F。
